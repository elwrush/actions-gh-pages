Fortunately

Phoneme /ˈfɔː.tʃən.ət.li/
Context "Fortunately, I caught the last train before the station closed."

Surprisingly

Phoneme /səˈpraɪ.zɪŋ.li/
Context "Surprisingly, the small boy finished the giant pizza by himself."

Amazingly

Phoneme /əˈmeɪ.zɪŋ.li/
Context "Amazingly, the seed grew into a giant tree in just one week."

Clearly

Phoneme /ˈklɪə.li/
Context "Clearly, he will not be able to lift 200kg."

Remarkably

Phoneme /rɪˈmɑː.kə.bli/
Context "Remarkably, the cat found its way home from 100 miles away."

Generously

Phoneme /ˈdʒen.ər.əs.li/
Context "Generously, he shared his only sandwich with the hungry dog."

STRATEGY

Supporting Your Opinion

  • Opinion: Messi is a great player.
  • Fact: Messi has won 8 Ballon d'Ors.
When you express an opinion, give real-life examples and facts to support it.
Compare 'Messi is good' vs 'Messi has won 8 Ballon d'Ors.' Which proves greatness?
