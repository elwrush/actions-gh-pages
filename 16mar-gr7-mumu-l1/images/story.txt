Meeting the Lady and Gerasim
An old lady lived on a quiet street in Moscow. Her house had big columns and was full of servants and companions.
  

* The Lady: An old, rich widow. She is a cruel, bossy, and moody woman who does whatever she wants. Her daughters got married a long time ago. Now, she lives alone in her "boring and greedy old age." Her days are dark and unhappy, but her evenings are even darker.
The most remarkable person in her house was Gerasim.
  

* Gerasim: The yard-keeper. He was born deaf and mute (he cannot hear or speak). He is not old, very tall (195 cm), strong, kind, and loyal. He works hard, is very serious, and loves order.
The lady brought him from her village. There, Gerasim lived in a small wooden house away from his brothers and was the best worker. Because he grew up in the countryside, he missed it very much and had a hard time getting used to city life. The work in the city was too easy for him. After finishing everything in half an hour, he would throw himself on the ground and lie there for hours, sad and completely still, "like a trapped animal."
Soon, Gerasim got used to his new home. He did his job well, kept the yard clean, and local thieves stayed away from the house. The other servants were a bit afraid of the huge deaf man, but Gerasim didn't hurt them. He treated them like friends and learned to talk to them using hand signs.
He lived in a small room above the kitchen. He built strong furniture for himself, strong enough for a giant like him. He always locked his door and didn't like it when people came into his room.

The Lady Separates Gerasim from the Woman He Loves
A year passed. The lady, who had total power over her servants, decided to marry off Kapiton Klimov.
  

* Kapiton Klimov: The lady’s shoemaker. He is a heavy drinker who thinks he is educated and unappreciated by the world.
The lady thought marriage would make him stop drinking. She chose Tatiana to be his wife and ordered Gavrila to organize the wedding.
  

* Tatiana: The lady’s washerwoman. She is 28 years old, thin, blonde, shy, and scared of everything.
  

* Gavrila Andreich: The lady’s butler. He is a coward, sneaky, and tries too hard to please the lady. He has yellow eyes and a duck-like nose.
Tatiana only had a few old uncles far away in the village, so nobody could protect her. Since childhood, she worked very hard and wore old clothes. She used to be beautiful, but the hard work quickly took her beauty away. She was easily frightened, didn't care about herself, and only wanted to finish her work on time.
Gerasim liked Tatiana. He awkwardly showed his feelings for her and protected her from bullies, but she was very afraid of the huge deaf man. The lady heard about Gerasim's feelings, but the "strange old woman" just laughed and gave him a reward. Gerasim was a little afraid of the lady, but he still wanted to ask her permission to marry Tatiana.
Gavrila thought hard about the problem: the lady liked Gerasim, but how could a deaf and mute man be a husband? Kapiton wanted to get married, but he was scared of the strong yard-keeper and called him a "beast." Tatiana simply accepted the lady's order. She only felt weak for a moment and leaned against a doorframe.
The butler hoped the lady would forget her idea, but she asked about the wedding every day. Finally, Gavrila remembered that Gerasim hated drunk people. He made a sneaky plan: he told Tatiana to pretend to be drunk and walk past Gerasim. The trick worked—Gerasim turned away from Tatiana in disgust, and she married the shoemaker.
Another year passed. Kapiton became a complete alcoholic, so the lady sent him and Tatiana away to a far village. Before they left, Gerasim gave Tatiana a red scarf he had bought for her a year ago. Tatiana cried and kissed Gerasim three times. The deaf man started to walk with her to say goodbye, but turned back halfway.

Gerasim Saves a Little Dog and Finds a Friend
Walking back home along the river, Gerasim saw a drowning puppy. He pulled it out of the water, took it to his room, and nursed it back to health.
"No mother takes care of her child the way Gerasim took care of his little pet."
The puppy grew into a very pretty dog. Gerasim named her Mumu: it was the only word he could say.
* Mumu: A small dog with long ears, a fluffy tail, and big, expressive eyes. She is very smart and loyal.
Mumu was very sweet. Gerasim loved her with all his heart, and the other servants loved her too. The dog followed the deaf man everywhere, guarded the yard at night, and never barked for no reason. She never went inside the lady's house.
________________


The Lady Meets Mumu
A year later, the lady looked out the window and saw Mumu. She was in a "happy mood" that day. She laughed, joked, and wanted her companions to do the same. But they were afraid: her good moods never lasted long and usually ended in dark, angry moods.
The lady liked Mumu and ordered the servants to bring the dog to her room. But Mumu was scared of the strange place and people. She hid in a corner, growled at the old woman, and showed her teeth. The lady's mood quickly turned bad, and she told them to take Mumu away.
The next morning, the lady woke up in a terrible mood. She said a dog's barking kept her awake all night and ordered Gavrila to get rid of Mumu. Gavrila told a servant to sell the dog at the market.
Gerasim quickly noticed his best friend was missing. He asked the servants about her. Many knew what happened, but they didn't tell him and only laughed. Gerasim stopped working and looked for Mumu everywhere, but couldn't find her. He came home tired and brokenhearted, and this time, nobody laughed at him.
Gerasim became deeply depressed. But a day later, the dog came back to him with a broken piece of rope around her neck!
