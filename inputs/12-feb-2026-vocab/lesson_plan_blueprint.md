# Lesson Blueprint: 12-02-2026-B1-FITTING-IN-LP

**Lesson Title**: 12-02-2026-B1-Fitting-In-LP
**Objective**: By the end of the lesson, learners will be better able to use vocabulary related to peer pressure and correctly form nouns from verbs in the context of personal identity and social groups.
**Systems/Skills**: Vocabulary / Noun & Verb Formation
**CEFR Level**: B1
**Teacher**: Ed Rush
**Duration**: 45 minutes
**Materials**: Oxford Discover Futures 3 Workbook p6, Reveal.js Presentation
**Shape**: G (Task-Based Learning)

---

### **Lesson Stages**

| Stage | Aim | Procedure | Time | Interaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-Task** | To activate schemata and activate the concept of 'Genius' | **Lisa (Blackpink)**: Show a photo of Lisa with the caption: "What makes her an entertainment genius?" Pairs brainstorm qualities (talent, hard work, uniqueness). T elicits ideas and bridges to Einstein. | 7 | Ss-Ss / T-Ss |
| **Task Cycle** | To provide a reason for using the target language. | **The Genius Text**: Ss read the Albert Einstein text (Task 2). In pairs, they choose the correct words to complete the text. **Challenge**: Ss find one thing Einstein "rebelled" against. | 10 | Ss-Ss |
| **Planning** | To prepare for reporting and focus on accuracy. | **Noun/Verb Focus**: Ss look at Tasks 3 & 4. Groups complete texts about Stella Young and Emotions, focusing on the correct form (noun vs. verb). T monitors. | 8 | Ss-Ss / T |
| **Report** | To share findings and compare answers. | **Check & Share**: Groups compare answers. T reveals correct answers. Ss discuss: "Which text was more surprising—Einstein or Stella Young?" | 5 | Ss-Ss / T-Ss |
| **Analysis** | To clarify meaning, form, and pronunciation of TL. | **The Grid**: T displays a table of verbs and nouns. Ss identify suffixes (-ance, -ion, -ing). **Drill**: Choral drilling of word stress (e.g., 'RE-act' vs 're-AC-tion'). | 10 | T-Ss |
| **Post-Task** | To provide freer practice / recycling. | **Personal Sentences**: Ss write 3 sentences about themselves using one noun and one verb form from the lesson. Share with a partner. | 5 | S / Ss-Ss |

**Total Time**: 45 minutes
