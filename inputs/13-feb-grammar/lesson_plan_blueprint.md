# Lesson Plan Blueprint: Grammar (used to vs would)

[APPROVED]

**Gold Standard Model:** `C:\PROJECTS\LESSONS AND SLIDESHOWS 2\inputs\26-Jan-Sentence-Boundaries\26-01-2026-LP-B1-Sentence-Boundaries-C.typ` (Adapted for strict Shape C compliance)

**Shape:** C (Test-Teach-Test)
**CEFR:** B1
**Duration:** 45 Minutes

## Stages:
1. **Lead-in** (5 Min): To engage Ss and activate schemata about past habits.
   - T writes "When I was 10, I..." on the board and elicits 3 things they did as children that they don't do now. Ss discuss in pairs.
2. **Test 1** (10 Min): To test prior knowledge of past habits using 'used to' and 'would'.
   - Ss complete Exercise 5 (Complete the dialogue) and Exercise 7 (Replace used to with would where possible) from the workbook.
3. **Teach** (10 Min): To clarify meaning, form, and pronunciation of target language.
   - T reviews answers on the board.
   - Clarify the difference between 'used to' (states and habits) and 'would' (action verbs/habits only, NOT states like "live" or "be").
   - Highlight pronunciation of "used to" /juːst tʊ/.
4. **Test 2** (10 Min): To test understanding of the clarified rules.
   - Ss complete Exercise 8 (Complete sentences with used to / didn't use to or would / wouldn't).
   - Ss swap and check in pairs. T monitors and corrects common errors.
5. **Practice** (10 Min): To provide freer practice.
   - Ss think of a major change in their life (like starting a new school, or moving, referencing Ex 12).
   - Ss write 3 sentences about their past habits vs now, using both 'used to' and 'would'. They share with their group.
