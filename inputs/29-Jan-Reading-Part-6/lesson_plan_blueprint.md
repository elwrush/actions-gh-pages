## Aim
By the end of the lesson, learners will have practiced the sub-skills of scanning for specific information and reading for detail in the context of three legendary cities: Atlantis, El Dorado, and Shangri-La.

## Stages

### Stage 1: Lead-in (5 mins)
- **Activity**: "Legendary Cities: Fact or Fiction?"
- **Procedure**: Present 3 statements about Atlantis and El Dorado. Ss guess if they are true or false in pairs.
- **Feedback**: Brief class discussion about what they already know.

### Stage 2: Pre-teach Vocab (8 mins)
- **Target Items**: philosopher, earthquake, ritual, fictional, paradise.
- **Procedure**: 
    - Present words in English-only context sentences (e.g., "Plato was a famous Greek *philosopher*.")
    - Ss match words to definitions.
    - Drill pronunciation (stress: phi-LO-so-pher, EARTH-quake, RI-tu-al).

### Stage 3: Gist / Scanning (7 mins)
- **Activity**: Summary Completion (WB Ex 3).
- **Procedure**: 
    - Ss scan the text to fill the 10 gaps in the summary.
    - Set a 4-minute timer to encourage scanning.
    - Peer-check in pairs.

### Stage 4: Main Task (Detail) (15 mins)
- **Activity**: Multiple Choice & Underlining (WB Ex 4 & 5).
- **Procedure**:
    - Ss answer MCQ questions (Ex 5).
    - Ss underline the sentence in the text that gives the answer (Ex 4).
    - Feedback: "Where exactly in the text did you find the answer for El Dorado?"

### Stage 5: Post-task (10 mins)
- **Activity**: "Your Shangri-La" (WB Ex 6).
- **Procedure**:
    - Ss discuss their "dream city" in small groups.
    - Focus on: "What would it have? What would it definitely NOT have?"
    - Language Focus: Recycling adjectives (remot, beautiful, powerful).
