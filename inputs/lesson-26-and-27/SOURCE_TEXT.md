### Task 4: Choice of Subject (Count: 1)
"You are going to write a description of a person you admire. Choose one of the people or think of your own idea."
- A character from fiction (a book, TV show, or film)
- A person from history
- An athlete
- (Your idea)

### Task 5: Brainstorming (Count: 3 traits)
"Make notes that summarize why you admire the person you are going to write about."

### Task 6: Strategy & Research (Count: 2-3 facts)
"Read the writing strategy. Then search online to find out about the person's life. Write details that support what you wrote in exercise 5."

**Writing Strategy: Supporting your opinion**
"Many writing text types require you to express your personal opinions. When you express an opinion, give real-life examples and facts to support it."
- Example Opinion: Messi is a great player.
- Example Fact: Messi has won 8 Ballon d'Ors.

### Modeling: Lisa (Blackpink)
- Subject: Lisa (Blackpink)
- Admirable Trait: Hard-working
- Supporting Fact: She trained for 5,300 hours before her debut.

### Task 7: Language Focus (Attitude Adverbs) (Count: 6 sentences)
"Use the attitude adverbs from exercise 2 to write at least six sentences about the person's life."
Expected items: 6 numbered response lines.

### Task 8: Writing (Count: 225 words)
"Write a first draft of your description of a person you admire. Write about 225 words. Use your answers from exercises 5-7 to help you."
- Point: Opinion | Checklist: State your opinion clearly
- Point: Adverbs | Checklist: Use attitude adverbs correctly
- Point: Facts | Checklist: Support every point with a fact

### Task 9: Review and edit
"Read your first draft. How can you improve it? Use the checklist to help you."

**Writing Checklist**:
- **Content**:
    - Have you clearly stated your opinion about the person you admire?
    - Have you supported your opinion with facts about their life?
    - Have you explained their achievements?
- **Language**:
    - Is your spelling correct?
    - Is your grammar correct?
    - Have you used attitude adverbs?

### Task 10: Final Draft
"Now write a final draft of your description of a person you admire."

## Answer Key

### Pre-teaching: Attitude Adverbs
1. **Amazingly**, the seed grew into a giant tree in just one week.
2. **Remarkably**, the cat found its way home from 100 miles away.
3. **Generously**, he shared his only sandwich with the hungry dog.
4. **Fortunately**, I caught the last train before the station closed.
5. **Clearly**, he will not be able to lift 200kg.
6. **Surprisingly**, the small boy finished the giant pizza by himself.

### Task 7: Attitude Adverbs (Suggested)
1. **Amazingly**, she won the gold medal despite her injury.
2. **Remarkably**, he survived the crash without any scratches.
3. **Generously**, they decided to donate all their money to charity.
4. **Fortunately**, he found his missing passport just before the flight.
5. **Clearly**, the team managed to win the game in the final minute.
6. **Surprisingly**, she has written ten books by the age of twenty.
