# Lesson Plan Blueprint: Someone I Admire (Unit 4)

**Topic**: Someone I Admire (Descriptive Writing)
**Shape**: F (Productive Skills)
**Level**: B1
**Duration**: 45 Minutes
**Model**: `inputs\archive 2026\29-01-2026-B2-Sports-Interviews-F-LP.typ`
**Source Folder**: `inputs/lesson-26-and-27`

---

### Stage 1: Lead-in (6 mins)
- **Goal**: To activate schema and engage students with the concept of "admirable" figures.
- **Activity**: "The Adjective Match." T displays images of a fictional character, a historical figure, and an athlete (matching **Task 4**). Ss brainstorm 2 adjectives for each person.
- **Feedback**: Elicit: "Who is someone YOU admire? Why?"
- **Interaction**: T-Ss / Ss-Ss

### Stage 2: Preparation & Input (12 mins)
- **Goal**: To introduce the "Supporting your opinion" strategy and "Attitude Adverbs" language focus.
- **Activity**:
    - **Part 1: Strategy Focus (Task 6)**: T presents the "Writing Strategy: Supporting your opinion." Ss discuss why facts and examples are better than just opinions.
    - **Part 2: Language Input (Task 7)**: T introduces 5 key attitude adverbs from the slideshow (*Amazingly, Surprisingly, Remarkably, Fortunately, Clearly*).
    - **Mini-task**: Ss transform simple sentences into "attitude" sentences (e.g., "He won the race" -> "*Remarkably*, he won the race"). T checks meaning and models word stress (*re-MARK-ab-ly*).
- **Interaction**: T-Ss / Indiv

### Stage 3: Preparation for Output (8 mins)
- **Goal**: To brainstorm and plan the content using the Workbook prompts.
- **Activity**:
    - **Part 1 (Task 4/5)**: Ss select their specific person (fictional, historical, athlete, or personal) and make notes in their WB (**Task 5**) on why they admire the person.
    - **Part 2 (Task 6)**: Ss identify at least two specific facts or achievements from the person's life that support their opinion. T monitors and provides target vocabulary.
- **Interaction**: Indiv

### Stage 4: Productive Task (19 mins)
- **Goal**: To produce the 225-word first draft using the models and language from Stages 2-3.
- **Activity**:
    - **Part 1 (Task 8)**: Ss write their first draft of the description (approx. 225 words). T starts a 15-minute timer on the slideshow.
    - **Part 2**: T monitors specifically for the inclusion of attitude adverbs (**Task 7**) and supporting facts (**Task 6**).
    - **Part 3**: Wrap-up. Ss read their "Closing Statement" aloud to a partner to check for a strong finish.
- **Interaction**: Indiv / Ss-Ss
