# Lesson Plan Blueprint: Lesson 23 - What makes a hero? (Relative Clauses & Emergencies)

## Metadata
*   **Teacher:** Ed Rush
*   **Date:** 04-03-2026
*   **Level:** B1
*   **Duration:** 45 Minutes
*   **Shape:** C (Test-Teach-Test)
*   **Focus:** Grammar (Relative Clauses) & Vocabulary (Emergencies)
*   **Materials:** Oxford Discover Futures 3 Workbook Unit 4 (p. 37-38)

## Stage 1: Lead-in (5 mins)
*   **Goal:** To activate schemata about heroes and emergencies.
*   **Activity:** "Hero or Not?" T shows 3 scenarios (e.g., finding a wallet, saving a cat, helping a friend with homework). Ss vote using icons.
*   **Interaction:** T-Ss / Ss-Ss

## Stage 2: Test 1 (10 mins)
*   **Goal:** To test prior knowledge of relative pronouns and emergency vocabulary.
*   **Activity:**
    *   **Vocab:** Task 10 (Matching definitions for emergency phrases).
    *   **Grammar:** Task 5 (Nasreddin Hodja text - choosing the correct relative pronoun).
*   **Interaction:** Individual -> Peer Check

## Stage 3: Teach (10 mins)
*   **Goal:** To clarify the rules of Defining vs. Non-defining relative clauses and when to omit pronouns.
*   **Clarification:**
    *   **Defining vs. Non-defining:** Use the Nasreddin example vs. Roald Amundsen (Task 7).
    *   **Omission Rule:** Pronouns can be omitted when they are the *object* of the clause (Task 8 analysis).
    *   **Meaning:** "Who" (people), "Which/That" (things), "Where" (places), "When" (time), "Whose" (possession).
*   **Interaction:** T-Ss (Interactive presentation)

## Stage 4: Test 2 (10 mins)
*   **Goal:** To provide controlled practice with non-defining clauses and complex sentences.
*   **Activity:**
    *   **Grammar:** Task 7 (Combining sentences using non-defining clauses - Rosa Parks, Marie Curie).
    *   **Vocabulary:** Task 11 (Completing the 'Heroic Friend' story with context words).
*   **Interaction:** Individual -> Class Feedback

## Stage 5: Production / Consolidation (10 mins)
*   **Goal:** To provide freer practice and personalization.
*   **Activity:**
    *   **Task 6 (Variation):** Ss write their own "Guess the Hero" riddle using at least 3 relative clauses.
    *   **Speaking:** Ss read their riddles to their partners/groups.
    *   **Final Discussion:** "What makes a hero?" (Task 12).
*   **Interaction:** Individual -> Ss-Ss
