## Grammar: Relative Clauses (Defining and Non-defining)

### Task 5: Nasreddin Hodja (Defining Relative Clauses)
*   **Text:** Nasreddin Hodja was a wise man and storyteller **who** lived in the 13th century. He's famous because there are thousands of stories **where** he's a kind of comic hero. In one story, he searches for a ring **which** he had lost in his house. After searching briefly inside the house, **where** he's sure the ring is, he goes to look outside. His wife asks him, "Why are you looking outside? You lost the ring inside the house!" Without stopping his search, Nasreddin answers, "Because this is **where** the light is the best - I can see better here!" Why do we call someone **who** makes such a silly mistake a comic hero? Because even at times **when** he makes silly mistakes, he believes in himself. Comic heroes always manage to show us the funny side of situations **which** make most people angry or upset.

### Task 7: Non-defining Relative Clauses
*   **Example:** Roald Amundsen, **who was from Norway**, reached the South Pole on December 14, 1911.
1.  **Rosa Parks:** Rosa Parks, **who is known as "the first lady of civil rights"**, refused to give up her seat to a white man on a bus.
2.  **Shakespeare:** Shakespeare's plays, **which are among the world's most famous**, influenced the English language. (Alternative based on prompt: Shakespeare's plays, **one of whose authors is among the world's most famous playwrights**, influenced... No, simpler: Shakespeare, **whose plays influenced the English language**, is one of the world's most famous playwrights.) -> *Correction based on prompt*: Shakespeare's plays influenced the English language. He is one of the world's most famous playwrights. -> Shakespeare, **whose plays influenced the English language**, is one of the world's most famous playwrights. OR Shakespeare's plays, **which influenced the English language**, are famous. Let's stick to the prompt structure.
    *   Prompt: Shakespeare's plays influenced the English language. He is one of the world's most famous playwrights. (whose)
    *   Answer: Shakespeare, **whose plays influenced the English language**, is one of the world's most famous playwrights.
3.  **Irena Szewińska:** Irena Szewińska, **who is from Poland**, is the only athlete ever to hold the world record for the 100 m, 200 m, and 400 m race at the same time. (where) -> *Correction based on prompt*: Irena Szewińska, from Poland, is the only athlete... She is a national hero in Poland. (where) -> Irena Szewińska is a national hero in Poland, **where she is the only athlete...**
4.  **Marie Curie:** Marie Curie was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize. She won the prize in 1903. (which)
    *   Answer: Marie Curie, **who won the Nobel Prize in 1903**, was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize. OR Marie Curie was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize, **which she won in 1903**.

### Task 8: Omitting Pronouns
*   1. **where** (cannot be omitted)
*   2. **who** (cannot be omitted - subject)
*   3. **who** (cannot be omitted - subject)
*   4. **that** (CAN be omitted - object: "everything you wouldn't have done")
*   5. **when** (cannot be omitted)
*   6. **who** (CAN be omitted - object: "someone I know")
