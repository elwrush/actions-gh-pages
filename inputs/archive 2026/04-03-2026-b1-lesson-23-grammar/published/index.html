The Comma Rule

When to use extra information

GRAMMAR BRIDGE

COMMAS & EXTRA INFO

  • Defining: Necessary info. No commas! (e.g., The man who lived next door.)
  • Non-defining: Extra info. Use commas! (e.g., Rosa Parks, who was brave, sat down.)
  • Omission: You can hide the pronoun in Defining clauses only.
Discuss the strategy with students.
