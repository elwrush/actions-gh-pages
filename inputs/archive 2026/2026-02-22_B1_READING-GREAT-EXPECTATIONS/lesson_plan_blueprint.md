# REFINED Blueprint: Great Expectations (B1 Reading) [APPROVED]
**Shape**: E (Receptive Skills) | **Model**: Gold Infographic (05-02-2026)

---

### Stage 1: Lead-in ("The Mystery Ship")
- **Goal**: Engage with an interactive hook and activate schemata.
- **Activity**: **Fact or Fiction?** Students vote on 3 statements about Victorian prison ships ("hulks").
- **Mission**: Brief Ss on the three mission objectives: Meet Dickens, Understand the Story, Discuss Themes.

### Stage 2: Pre-teach Vocab ("Barrier Removal")
- **Goal**: Remove lexical barriers before scanning.
- **Activity**: **Contextual Discovery**. Select 5 words: *Churchyard, Gravestone, Marsh, Blacksmith, File*.
    - Present with context sentences and match to definitions/visuals.
    - Model & Drill: Focus on word stress.

### Stage 3: Gist / Scanning ("The Sub-Skill Workout")
- **Goal**: Practice scanning for specifics.
- **Activity**: 
    - **Task 1 (Scanning)**: 90s timer to find 3 author facts from Section 1.
    - **Task 2 (Visual Prediction)**: Use the Probe-First Table to categorize feelings/sayings based on the scene image.
    - **Task 3 (Matching)**: Guess word/phrase associations (Man or Boy) based on the image.

### Stage 4: Main Task (Detail) ("The Audio Experience")
- **Goal**: Read for detail and emotion.
- **Activity**: **Detail Detective**.
    - **The Audio Experience**: Listen to the theatrical performance of the encounter.
    - **Task 4 (Split Revelation)**: 
        - Part 1: Recall questions (1. Type of man, 2. Pip's items, 3. Pip's actions).
        - Part 2: Reflection questions (4. Moral choice, 5. Prediction check).

### Stage 5: Post-task ("The Editing & Evidence Combo")
- **Goal**: Recycle language and practice analysis.
- **Activity**:
    - **Part 1 (Vocabulary Focus/Editing)**: Correct **7** mistakes in the summary paragraph using the sequential editing layout.
    - **Part 2 (Post-task/Analysis)**: 
        - **Task 6 (Assessment)**: Decide on 5 Agree/Disagree statements and find evidence.
        - **Discussion**: Explore themes using the O-R-E framework (Opinion-Reason-Example).
