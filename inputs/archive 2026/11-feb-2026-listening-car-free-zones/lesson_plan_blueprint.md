# Lesson Plan Blueprint: Car-Free Zones

## Metadata
- **Teacher**: Ed Rush
- **Date**: 11 February 2026
- **CEFR Level**: B1
- **Duration**: 45 minutes
- **Skill**: Listening (Identifying Advantages and Disadvantages)
- **Topic**: Car-Free Zones and Navigating a City Map
- **Shape**: E (Receptive Skills)
- **Objective**: By the end of the lesson, learners will have practiced identifying advantages and disadvantages in the context of a listening text about car-free zones and map navigation.

---

## Lesson Stages

### STAGE ONE: Lead-in
- **Aim**: To engage Ss and activate schemata.
- **Procedure**:
    1. **Map Challenge**: Show the map (WB p. 19). Ss identify the gray area (car-free zone).
    2. **Prediction**: Pairs discuss: "What are the pros and cons of no cars in the city center?" (Exercise 1).
    3. **Feedback**: T elicits ideas and writes on board.
- **Time**: 5 mins
- **Interaction**: T-Ss / Ss-Ss

### STAGE TWO: Pre-teach Vocab
- **Aim**: To remove linguistic barriers to comprehension.
- **Procedure**:
    1. **Visual Match**: T shows icons/images for *pedestrians*, *air pollution*, *handicapped*, and *carbon footprint*.
    2. **Concept Check**: CCQs: "Are pedestrians walking or driving?" "Is air pollution good for health?"
    3. **Drill**: Model and drill pronunciation.
- **Time**: 7 mins
- **Interaction**: T-Ss

### STAGE THREE: Gist / Scanning
- **Aim**: To provide a purpose for first listening (Global understanding).
- **Procedure**:
    1. **The Big Picture**: T sets task: "Listen once. Where is the girl going? What is the main topic of the conversation?"
    2. **Listen**: Play audio (ODF3 WB 2.01).
    3. **Check**: Pairs compare. T-Ss feedback (Destination: School/Town center; Topic: Car-free zones/Directions).
- **Time**: 5 mins
- **Interaction**: S / Ss-Ss

### STAGE FOUR: Main Task (Detail)
- **Aim**: To practice listening for specific information (Adv/Disadv).
- **Procedure**:
    1. **Data Collection**: Ss look at the chart (Exercise 2).
    2. **Listen Again**: Ss listen and note down the advantages and disadvantages mentioned by the Mom and Girl.
    3. **Peer Check**: Ss compare charts in pairs.
    4. **Class Feedback**: T elicits answers and clarifies any tricky points from the script.
- **Time**: 10 mins
- **Interaction**: S / Ss-Ss / T-Ss

### STAGE FIVE: Vocabulary Focus
- **Aim**: To focus on useful phrases for map navigation.
- **Procedure**:
    1. **Map Talk**: Ss complete Exercise 4 (Choose the correct word) individually.
    2. **Dialogue Build**: Ss use the phrases to complete the dialogue in Exercise 5.
    3. **Verify**: Ss listen one last time to check their dialogue answers.
- **Time**: 10 mins
- **Interaction**: S / T-Ss

### STAGE SIX: Post-task
- **Aim**: To provide freer practice and personalize the topic.
- **Procedure**:
    1. **Roleplay**: Pairs choose a situation from Exercise 6 (e.g., getting to the theater or beach).
    2. **Create**: Ss write a short dialogue using the map and navigation phrases.
    3. **Perform**: Selected pairs act out their dialogue for the class.
- **Time**: 8 mins
- **Interaction**: Ss-Ss / T-Ss
