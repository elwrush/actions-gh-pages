TASK 2

THE ADVANTAGE AUDIT

  • ACTIVE LISTENING: Listen for words like 'nice', 'safe', or 'healthier' to spot advantages.
  • SIGNAL WORDS: Watch out for 'but', 'hard', or 'difficult'—these often signal a disadvantage.
  • SUMMARIZE: Don't write every word! Use short phrases (e.g., 'no cars = quiet') to fill your chart.

TASK 3

MASTERING PREPOSITIONS

  • LANDMARK FOCUS: Identify the building first (e.g., school), then find the preposition that connects it to the road.
  • PATH TRACKING: Use 'ALONG' for movement on a road and 'FOR' when scanning the horizon for a specific target.
  • DIRECTIONAL CUES: Listen for 'IN' to specify the general orientation before confirming the exact spot.
Discuss the strategy with students.
