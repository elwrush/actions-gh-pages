## Audio Script (ODF3 WB 2.01)
**Mom:** We're so glad to have you here.
**Girl:** It's great to be here.
**Mom:** Now, I know you'll have a map on your phone and you won't have any problems getting around, but I just wanted to show you the best way to get into town.
**Girl:** Great. Thank you.
**Mom:** When you go into town for your classes, you'll get off the bus here, at the central bus station. To get to the school, you go east along this road and look out for the school, on your left. It's marked here with a symbol of a shopping cart.
**Girl:** And what does this symbol mean?
**Mom:** That's the swimming pool. If you get to the swimming pool, you've gone too far!
**Girl:** OK. So ... after the supermarket ...
**Mom:** Well, if you go in this direction – and continue on a little past the supermarket – you can't miss the school on your right.
**Girl:** OK, great. So, what is that gray area?
**Mom:** That's the car-free zone.
**Girl:** Car-free zone?
**Mom:** Yeah – the roads are closed to cars. You can't drive there.
**Girl:** Wow – that's cool. So, are those the shopping streets?
**Mom:** Yes, that's right. And being car-free makes the area really nice for walking. It's much safer for pedestrians with no cars there. I think you'll really like it.
**Girl:** It sounds great. We have an area like that in my city, too. I really love it. It's a lot quieter without cars. You don't always realize it, or think about it, but traffic is really loud – it makes a lot of noise.
**Girl:** Right. But, not everybody likes it, right?
**Mom:** What do you mean?
**Girl:** Well, my uncle uses a wheelchair, and drives a special car – just with his hands. When he needs to go out, he can park in a handicapped parking space, get his wheelchair out, and go where he needs to go. But he can't go into the car-free zone.
**Mom:** Yeah, that's true. For people who really need a car to get around, I guess it isn't so good. But the center of town center is healthier, but on the other hand, if you can't walk at all, then it means you'll have real problems in the car-free zone.
**Mom:** Yeah, and what about people with small children?
**Mom:** Yeah, that can be difficult. Imagine trying to drive with two or three small kids might have a hard time not being able to drive – and they might have trouble walking a lot, or getting on and off the bus.
**Girl:** Still, I guess for most people, the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.
**Mom:** Yeah, when you think about that parent and young kids, they'll appreciate the cleaner air in the car-free zone – there isn't nearly as much air pollution in the streets in that part of town.
**Girl:** That's true. Anyway, I look forward to going there.
**Mom:** There are a couple of other things I'd like to show you . . .

## Answer Key

- **Advantages**: nice for walking; safer for pedestrians; quieter without cars; center is healthier; air is cleaner (less air pollution).
- **Disadvantages**: not good for people who need a car to get around (e.g., wheelchair users); could be difficult for people with small kids (walking a lot/buses).
