# Visual Plan: Car-Free Zones

## Theme: The Urban Navigator
**Visual Style**: Clean, modern city map aesthetic with blue and orange accents. High-quality urban photography showing pedestrians and quiet city streets.

## Slide 1: THE MAIN MENU
- **Layout**: title
- **Content**: THE BEST WAY TO TOWN. Navigating the Car-Free Zone. Background video `images/city_timelapse.mp4`.

## Slide 2: YOUR MISSION
- **Layout**: mission
- **Content**: "YOUR MISSION" with `images/mission_bg_clipped.mp4`.
- **Items**: 
    - **MAP THE ZONE**: Identify the boundaries and benefits of a car-free city center.
    - **DECODE THE PROS & CONS**: Listen for the hidden advantages and challenges.
    - **NAVIGATE THE STREETS**: Master the language of directions and landmarks.
