# REFINED Blueprint: The Time Machine (B1 Reading) [APPROVED]
**Shape**: E (Receptive Skills) | **Model**: Gold Infographic (Standard)

---

### Phase 1: Lead-in ("The Father of Sci-Fi")
- **Goal**: Contextualize the author and the genre.
- **Activity**: **"Scanning for Facts"**.
- **Steps**:
    1. Show H.G. Wells profile slide (Task 1).
    2. Ss scan for: Publication Date (1895), Concept (Time Machine), Co-founder (Jules Verne).
    3. T highlights: Wells predicted the future (Atomic Bomb, Internet) in 1895.
- **Interaction**: T-Ss / Solo | **Time**: 5 Mins

### Phase 2: Reading / Listening ("Entering the Machine")
- **Goal**: Gist and Detail Understanding via Audio Reading.
- **Activity**: **"True, False, or Not Mentioned?"** (Task 2).
- **Steps**:
    1. Play audio (ODF2_SB_2.26.mp3) while students follow the text.
    2. Ss answer 5 True/False/NM questions.
        - Q1: Dinner frequency? -> **FALSE** (Weekly, not fortnightly).
        - Q2: Mandatory time project? -> **FALSE** (Implied choice).
        - Q3: Mixed reactions? -> **TRUE** (Wow vs Useful?).
        - Q4: Prototype built first? -> **TRUE**.
        - Q5: Tells story next visit? -> **NOT MENTIONED** (Scene ends).
    3. Feedback / Peer Check.
- **Interaction**: Solo -> Ss-Ss | **Time**: 12 Mins

### Phase 3: Character Analysis ("Case Files")
- **Goal**: Deep processing of character traits.
- **Activity**: **"Who Said What?"** (Task 3).
- **Steps**:
    1. Ss identify the character behind specific attitudes/lines.
    2. **Key**: 1. Mother (Vague), 2. Kai (Critical), 3. Jasmine (Observant), 4. Dan (Serious), 5. Maya (Breaks news).
- **Interaction**: Solo -> Pairs | **Time**: 8 Mins

### Phase 4: Text Structure ("Timeline Logic")
- **Goal**: Understanding narrative flow and scene management.
- **Activity**: **"Match the Scene"** (Task 4).
- **Steps**:
    1. Ss match Scene 1-4 with summary descriptions.
    2. **Key**: 
        - Scene 1: Discussion of projects.
        - Scene 2: Small machine demo (Basement).
        - Scene 3: Discussing the prototype (Specifics).
        - Scene 4: Dan returns (The climax).
- **Interaction**: Pairs | **Time**: 8 Mins

### Phase 5: Discussion ("The Verdict")
- **Goal**: Critical thinking and personalization.
- **Activity**: **"Brave or Foolish?"**.
- **Steps**:
    1. Debate: Was Dan right to risk his life for science?
    2. **Sides**: Brave Pioneer (Progress requires risk) vs. Reckless Fool (He worried his family).
    3. Vote on the "Verdict".
- **Interaction**: Groups -> Class | **Time**: 13 Mins

---
**Total Time**: 46 Minutes
