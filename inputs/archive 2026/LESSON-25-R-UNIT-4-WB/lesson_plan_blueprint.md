# Lesson Plan Blueprint: What Makes a Hero? (Lesson 25)

**Status**: [DRAFT]
**Shape**: E (Receptive Skills)
**Model**: Shape E Gold Standard (Infographic/Story Workout)

## 1. Lead-in: Hero or Not? (5 mins)
- **Activity**: Interactive Quiz. T shows images of 3 people (e.g., a famous athlete, a local volunteer, a scientist). Ss vote: "Is this a hero?" 
- **Goal**: Engage Ss and elicit the definition of a "hero" vs. an "unsung hero."

## 2. Pre-teach Vocab: Heroic Terms (8 mins)
- **Target Vocabulary**:
    1. *recognition* (noun) - public praise or thanks for something you have done.
    2. *determination* (noun) - the ability to continue trying to do something, although it is very difficult.
    3. *expedition* (noun) - an organized journey for a particular purpose, especially to find out about a place that is not well known.
    4. *courageous* (adjective) - having or showing courage; brave.
    5. *achievement* (noun) - something very good and difficult that you have succeeded in doing.
- **Activity**: Contextual matching. Ss match words to context sentences. Pronunciation drill focusing on word stress (e.g., de-ter-mi-**NA**-tion).

## 3. Gist / Scanning: Who's Who? (7 mins)
- **Activity**: Timed Speed Scan (2 mins). Ss scan the text to match the three names (Junko, Svetlana, Scott) to their "world" (The Mountain, The Stars, The Screen).
- **Feedback**: Ss compare in pairs. T asks: "How did you find the names so quickly?" (Headings).

## 4. Main Task: Fact vs. Opinion (15 mins)
- **Activity**: Detailed Reading. 
    - Ss complete WB Exercise 2: Identify Fact (F) vs. Opinion (O) for the 8 highlighted sentences.
    - Ss complete WB Exercise 4: True (T) / False (F) statements.
- **Feedback**: Peer check then whole class feedback with evidence from the text.

## 5. Post-task: The Heroic Language Focus (10 mins)
- **Part 1: Language Focus**: Word Building. Ss find noun forms of *courageous* (courage), *achieve* (achievement), *lead* (leadership), *determine* (determination) in the text.
- **Part 2: Personalization**: "Your Unsung Hero". Ss discuss in pairs: "Who is an unsung hero in your life or community? What makes them heroic?"
