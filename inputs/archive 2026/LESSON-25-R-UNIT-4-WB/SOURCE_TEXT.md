UNSUNG HEROES
Reading to learn
Heroes are recognized as courageous
leaders - people who help others or
make the world a better place. We read
about them in the news. They receive
awards. Unsung heroes, on the other
hand, achieve great things, but don't
receive much recognition for their
actions. I'd like to tell you about three
unsung heroes, and hopefully you'll
agree that people should know more
about them.
THE MOUNTAIN CLIMBER
When she was very young, Junko Tabei
(1939-2016) was not strong or healthy.
But she had a dream: to climb mountains.
Growing up, she had few opportunities
to climb. But then she joined the climbing
club at her college, and by the time she
graduated, she was an excellent climber.
However, many male climbers refused to
allow her to join their expeditions, mainly
because she was a woman. As a result, she
started the Ladies Climbing Club in Japan
in 1969 and began organizing all-female
expeditions. In 1975, she became the first
woman to climb Mount Everest, the world's
tallest peak. And she continued climbing
until she'd been to the top of the highest
mountain on all seven continents - the
so-called "seven summits." She was the first
woman to do so. 2Too little is known about
Tabei around the world. She showed great
determination, and her life in the mountains
was amazing. 3Such a courageous climber
should be a world-famous hero!
