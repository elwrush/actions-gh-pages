## SECTION 1: ABOUT THE NOVEL (Background Text)

[Para 1] *The Time Machine* was published in 1895. It was the first story to popularize the idea of time travel using a machine, and it created the expression "time machine" we use today. H. G. Wells, along with the French writer Jules Verne (1828–1905), is considered one of the creators of the modern science fiction genre. After *The Time Machine*, Wells wrote *The Invisible Man* (1897), *The War of the Worlds* (1898), and *The First Men in the Moon* (1901), which have all had an influence on modern science fiction novels and movies.

### Task 1: Comprehension
1. When was the novel *The Time Machine* published?
   **Answer: 1895 [Para 1]**
2. What did *The Time Machine* help make popular in the late 1800s?
   **Answer: the idea of time travel using a machine [Para 1]**
3. Which two writers developed the genre of modern science fiction?
   **Answer: H. G. Wells and Jules Verne [Para 1]**

## SECTION 2: THE PLAY SCRIPT (Primary Text)

### [Scene 1] Outside Dan's House & The Lounge
*Four young people: Kai, Amy, Maya, and Jasmine are outside their friend Dan's front door. Kai knocks.*

[S1:L1] **Dan's mother:** (*opening front door*) Hello everybody! Come in. Sit down. (*They start walking in and sit in the lounge.*) Dan! (*shouting*) Your school friends are here!
[S1:L2] **Amy:** Thank you for letting us come over.
[S1:L3] **Maya:** Yes, we love meeting here for pizza and to hang out every week.
[S1:L4] **Mother:** (*while they are waiting for Dan*) So what are you all doing for your school design projects?
[S1:L5] **Kai:** Well, I'm designing an app to help students organize their study time.
[S1:L6] **Mother:** I think Dan's idea has something to do with time, too. He's been working on it in the basement for months. Ah, here he is.
[S1:L7] **Dan:** Hi there, everybody!
[S1:L8] **Everybody:** Hey, Dan!
[S1:L9] **Maya:** Dan! Have you been working on your design for months? I've only just started mine!
[S1:L10] **Kai:** We know yours will be great, Dan, because you won first prize last year! What's your big idea this time?
[S1:L11] **Dan:** Well, it's complicated. (*scratching his head*) I've been reading about relativity, and looking at the latest microchips online, and ... (*pauses*)
[S1:L12] **Everybody:** (*all looking at Dan*) And?
[S1:L13] **Dan:** Let's go down to the basement. I'll show you.

### [Scene 2] The Basement
[S2:L1] **Dan:** Come over to the table. (*all move and stand round table*) Here's the design prototype.
[S2:L2] **Jasmine:** Wow, you've been busy!
[S2:L3] **Dan:** Yes! My model has a small circuit board, a digital time display, a seat to sit on, and a special lever that you can move backwards or forwards.
[S2:L4] **Amy:** Oh, cool! That's amazing. But what *is* it, Dan?
[S2:L5] **Dan:** It's my design for a time machine.
[S2:L6] **Kai:** A time machine? Don't we need to design something ... well ... useful?
[S2:L7] **Maya:** And make something that really works?
[S2:L8] **Dan:** Well, I'm building a full-sized time machine over there. (*He points*) And it *will* work. Who wants to test it?
[S2:L9] **Maya:** Me! I'll do it.
[S2:L10] **Dan:** Put your hand here ... . Ready? Now, push the lever forward. (*pause*)
[S2:L11] **Jasmine:** Look! The time on the clock is starting to go forwards!
[S2:L12] **Amy:** Oh ... it's ... it's disappearing! ... It's gone!
[S2:L13] **Dan:** Yes, it's traveled into a different time dimension.
[S2:L14] **Mother:** The pizzas are ready! (*shouting from upstairs*)
[S2:L15] **Dan:** (*sighs*) I suppose we'd better go upstairs.

### [Scene 3] After the Pizza (Upstairs)
[S3:L1] **Mother:** The pizzas are on the table... (*leaves room*)
[S3:L2] **Amy:** Hey, how did you do that, Dan? Tell us!
[S3:L3] **Kai:** It was some sort of trick.
[S3:L4] **Dan:** It wasn't a trick. The machine really has traveled forward in time.
[S3:L5] **Kai:** Do you really believe that, Dan?
[S3:L6] **Dan:** Yes. What's more, I've nearly finished the full-sized machine. I'm just waiting for one more part.
[S3:L7] **Amy:** And then what?
[S3:L8] **Dan:** I plan to make a trip into the future.
[S3:L9] **Kai:** Are you serious?
[S3:L10] **Dan:** I've never been more serious in my life.
[S3:L11] **Jasmine:** Oh, look at the time! I need to go home.
[S3:L12] **Maya:** Yes, it's time for us to go, too.
[S3:L13] **Jasmine:** Bye, Dan... Please be careful! See you next week.

### [Scene 4] One Week Later (The Return)
[S4:L1] **Mother:** Hi, everybody. Come in. Dan isn't here... I think he's left a note...
[S4:L2] **Maya:** (*reads note*) "Hi gang, sorry I'm not here. I'll be back at 6:30. Start eating without me..."
[S4:L3] **Kai:** He's just playing a joke on us. I'm sure.
[S4:L4] **Maya:** But the big time machine that he was building is gone. And there isn't any sign of Dan at all.
[S4:L5] **Jasmine:** Ah, maybe that's him! (*opens door*)
[S4:L6] **Pizza delivery person:** Hello there. Pizzas?
[S4:L7] **Amy:** Where is Dan? It's nearly 6:30. (*They eat silently.*)
[S4:L8] (*The sound of footsteps... Dan walks with difficulty... his hair is untidy, clothes slightly torn, no shoes.*)
[S4:L9] **Maya:** Dan! Where did you come from?
[S4:L10] **Amy:** You look terrible! And where are your shoes?
[S4:L11] **Dan:** (*sits down*) I ... I need something to eat.
[S4:L12] **Jasmine:** Here have this. (*Gives him pizza... he eats hungrily.*)
[S4:L13] **Kai:** What on earth have you been up to, Dan?
[S4:L14] **Dan:** Move closer ... and I'll tell you my story ... .

## SECTION 4: VOCABULARY & GLOSSARY

1. **Relativity**: Einstein’s theory... time is the fourth dimension.
2. **Prototype**: The first design of something.
3. **Circuit board**: A flat board which holds electrical circuits.
4. **Digital time display**: A clock with the time in digital numbers.
5. **Lever**: A handle used to operate a piece of machinery.
