Phoneme /dɪˌtɜː.mɪˈneɪ.ʃən/
Context "Even though her legs were tired and it was raining, her determination kept her running until she reached the finish line."

expedition

Phoneme /ˌek.spəˈdɪʃ.ən/
Context "The scientists packed their heavy gear and began their three-month expedition to the Amazon to study rare plants."

courageous

Phoneme /kəˈreɪ.dʒəs/
